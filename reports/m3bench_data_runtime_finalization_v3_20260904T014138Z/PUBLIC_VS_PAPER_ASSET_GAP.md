# Public versus paper asset gap

- The paper reports 16,276 questions.
- The runnable public T0-T4 catalog available to this audit contains 11,088 unique queries.
- The public release does not include model-specific base predictions, wrong-case selections, sample post-edit outputs, complete evaluation notebooks, or provider-specific Judge outputs.
- PMC-VQA is not present in the frozen public runnable catalog used here.
- T5 remains a separate blocked extension because the required PadChest-GR runnable assets are unavailable.
- Results from this workflow are **public-release-aligned**, never paper-exact or a reconstruction of the authors' private model-conditioned cohorts.
